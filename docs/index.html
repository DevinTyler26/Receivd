Receivd

TCG Order Tracker

Track what actually arrives

Receivd adds lightweight delivery tracking to authenticated TCGplayer order pages. Mark orders pending, delivered, partially delivered, missing, or refunded and reconcile received quantities for individual cards.

Receivd has no account or backend and never asks for TCGplayer credentials.
